# Design Context

Gathered from Rayhan directly (Oct 2026) for the motion redesign.

## Audience
- Big tech / AI lab recruiters: polish and clarity first; motion signals craft without slowing them down.
- Startups & founders: show that he ships.
- Peers: something memorable and shareable.

## Use cases
Skim who Rayhan is in seconds, see selected projects, get in touch.

## Personality
- **Cinematic & spacey**: slow ease-outs, light, depth. "The Horizon" is the through-line.
- **Kinetic typography**: big type that moves, splits, and fills in on scroll for key moments.
- **Editorial restraint**: everything else stays quiet. Motion only where it means something.

## Constraints
- Redesign, not replacement: keep the space theme, Space Grotesk + Inter, blue `#78b4e8` / orange `#ffb599` accents, and each page's existing background (tie them together, don't swap them).
- Scaled-down motion on phones (shorter distances, no cursor effects, horizontal gallery becomes a vertical stack).
- Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Motion system
- Signature: the preloader text collapses into a horizon line; the hero name rises out of it.
- Page transitions: horizon wipe (light line sweeps, new headline rises).
- Micro-interactions: subtle magnetic CTAs, cursor spotlight on cards, sliding nav indicator. No custom cursor, no text scramble.
- Engine: GSAP + ScrollTrigger on Locomotive Scroll v5 (Lenis), driven by `gsap.ticker`.

## Page concepts (Oct 2026 redesign)
- **About — chaptered story.** Near-black page; the loud About shader shows only through `.knockout` chapter titles (letters as windows) and floods the screen once, flying through the "E" of EXPERIENCE (rings race outward). Compact chapter rail on the left (labels on hover). Experience is a "focus stage": huge company index on the right sharpens at screen center, sticky stage on the left shows the focused role.
- **Contact — Transmission.** Shader rings stay anchored (no cursor-following — Rayhan disliked it), pulse per keystroke, retint per hovered channel, and burst on send; the message is typed in display type, collapses into a point of light on Transmit, and the visitor's mail app opens. Channels are tuned "frequencies" (email copies on click). No phone number on the site. Résumé link appears automatically when `public/resume.pdf` exists.
