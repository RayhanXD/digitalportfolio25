# Home: scroll-craft brief

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Rayhan asked for the homepage to be
revamped with scroll-craft ("we can do a lot better"), the hero kept as it is, and the rest
to be a creative design where "professional is def the #1 priority". Evidence below comes from
`.impeccable.md` (design context Rayhan gave in Oct 2026) and `EXPERIENCE_BANK.md`. Anything
not traced to those is an authored decision and is marked as such.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Cinematic, precise, quiet, confident. *(".impeccable.md": cinematic and spacey,
   kinetic type for key moments, editorial restraint everywhere else.)* Authored references: a
   film title sequence shot through a widescreen gate, a mission timeline, an annual report set
   with care.
2. **Journey.** The name on the horizon (kept hero), who he is in one sentence, where he has
   shipped, what he has built, what he builds with, how to reach him.
3. **Energy.** Calm open, quiet middle, loudest at the work, a release on the marquee, still
   at the close.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** See the curve and the peak below.
5. **One thing no other site does.** The horizon line that runs through the whole site opens
   like a camera shutter, and every project is shown through it.
6. **Range.** Premium-minimal with editorial type, cinematic but never noisy. Professional is
   the first priority (Rayhan, verbatim: "professional is def the #1 priority").
7. **One world or distinct scenes.** Distinct scenes over one continuous sky: the fixed planet
   footage never cuts, it regrades underneath every act.
8. **Assets.** Existing footage only: `campus-connect-ai.mp4`, `wave-lattice-card.mp4`,
   `agentic-search.mp4`, `projects-section-bg.mp4`. Nothing generated, no spend. The 130 MB
   `all-projects-bg.mp4` is no longer loaded on the homepage.

## Feeling curve

| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Hero (kept) | Awe | The name rises out of the horizon over the planet; stars and dust planes separate on scroll and pointer |
| 2 Statement | Clarity | One plain sentence brightens word by word at reading pace; the live "Currently" card beside it |
| 3 Experience | Trust | A month-by-month ruler slides under a still sun; each start date that reaches it raises the company out of the horizon, and lanes show the roles overlapping |
| 4 Selected work | **Wonder (peak)** | The line splits into a shutter and each project is shown through it, then closes back to a line |
| 5 Toolkit | Momentum | Two bands of type thrown faster by scroll speed, reversing when the scroll does |
| 6 Close | Resolve | The hero's composition returns: "Let's build" resting on the horizon while the sunrise comes back |

No two adjacent acts share a feeling. Act 3 is precise rather than loud (type and a ruler, no
footage), and the work act opens on a darkened stage, so the peak still has something to arrive from.

**Revision, after Rayhan's review:** the first Act 3 (four columns under a travelling sun) read as
"kinda a basic design idea". It is now the time ruler. The work shutter was also made smoother:
a double-buffered player so the next clip is decoded before the shutter opens, one continuous
close-into-open blink with light gathering on the line, copy that leaves before the edges close,
and more scroll per transition.

## The peak

> "The line across the middle of the screen split open like a shutter, and each project was inside it."

Act 4. It gets the footage budget (four clips, at most two buffered at a time), the silence in
front of it, and the most scroll room: 5 viewport-heights of pinned scroll on desktop, 4.2 on
phones, against 2.9 and 2.6 for the experience ruler.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where the horizon line opens like a camera shutter to show you each thing he built.

## Authored silence

Act 4 opens on a darkened, empty stage holding a single line for the first ~8% of its timeline
before the shutter opens, and the line holds alone again for a beat after the last project
closes. The background deepens to 0.92 dim around the stage at the same time. Both are intended,
not dead scroll.

## Grammar: filmic one-shot, and why the other seven lost

- **Chaptered editorial:** the About page already is chaptered (chapter rail, knockout titles).
  Two chaptered pages would make the site one page twice.
- **Live surface:** bans kinetic type, footage and marketing chrome, all of which are kept
  constraints, and there is no real product surface to run honestly.
- **Continuous world:** needs worldflight footage (no kie.ai key, no budget), and the brief is a
  career, not travel through a place.
- **Typographic poster:** bans a photographic ground; the planet footage is a kept constraint.
- **Gallery / catalog:** the Projects page is already the walkable gallery.
- **Split stage:** there is no two-sided argument here.
- **Rhythmic cutlist:** the wrong register for recruiters, and it bans the pin the peak needs.

One deliberate deviation: filmic forbids more than one entry point, but the work act carries a
project index that jumps between projects. It stays, because recruiters skim and professional
is the first priority. It names projects; it never numbers them.

## Score

| Beat | Device | Why this one |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | Layered parallax + pointer depth (kept hero) | Rayhan likes it as it is |
| Clarity | Kinetic word-brighten, scrubbed, in flow | Reading pace is the device |
| Trust | Pinned time ruler: a lane chart sliding under a fixed sun, kinetic names | Overlap and duration are real information; a ruler shows them honestly |
| Wonder | Pin + clip-path reveal (the shutter) | The signature move, and the longest pin |
| Momentum | Velocity-driven kinetic marquee | A release after the held peak |
| Resolve | Drift (background regrades to sunrise) + kinetic bookend | The ending arrives and holds |

Six device families, none twice in a row, no scrub video. Acts 3 and 4 both pin, but with
different devices (a sliding ruler, then a shutter). About 13.2 viewport-heights across six acts
at 1440×900, under the 13.6 to 13.8vh band.
